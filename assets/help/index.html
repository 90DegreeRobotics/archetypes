ARCHETYPES

You are the Witness: the sovereign eighth seat. The seven archetypes advise. They never replace you.

Modes

  • Standard Mode — Offer language to the AURA council. Three voices confer. A verdict collapses. Chronos paints the authorized image. Esc returns to the menu.
  • Consciousness — Sit with one archetype. Direct counsel, memory, and a painted reply.
  • Oracle Riddle — A hidden three-word vision. Reconstruct the prompt. Insight is the reward. A truth extracted in Inner Chambers can seed the next round.
  • Inner Chambers — Walk seven minds around a hub. Align with a luminous node and press E to extract a truth.
  • Living Engine — Tune three orbital resonances. Keep the Aura from starving or overloading. Viren is entropy; counter-frequency cures it.

Services

The Desktop launcher starts Ollama, Chronos Director, and ComfyUI when they are installed and down. Chronos remains a sibling NeuroCognica product; Archetypes does not download it. Council voices install beside the game.

Logs

%LOCALAPPDATA%\NeuroCognica\Archetypes\logs\last-failure.txt

%LOCALAPPDATA%\NeuroCognica\Archetypes\logs\last-engine.log

If the chamber will not open, that failure file is the truth. The launcher opens it in Notepad.

Uninstall

Start Menu → Uninstall Archetypes. Your Witness profile under LocalAppData can be kept.
